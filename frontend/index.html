The Process

From PDF to Insight in 4 Steps

Powered by a real-time RAG pipeline

01

Upload PDF

Drag & drop or browse your research paper. Supports any academic PDF up to 10 MB.

02

Chunking & Indexing

The document is split into semantic chunks, embedded with SentenceTransformers, and indexed in FAISS.

03

AI Analysis

Gemini 3.5 Flash & 3.1 Pro retrieve the most relevant chunks via RAG to generate grounded, accurate insights.

04

Chat & Export

Ask follow-up questions in the chat panel or download a polished PDF report.
